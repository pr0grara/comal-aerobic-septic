# Comal Aerobic Septic: anchor contractor shortlist

Researched 2026-09-22 (tunnel thread 808, one web-research agent, 83 page opens). Nothing here has
been verified by a human yet. Site: hillcountryaerobicseptic.com, tracking number (830) 500-5209.
At research time the site had 5 unique people all-time and 4 in the trailing 28 days. Goal: ONE
small Comal County aerobic maintenance provider on a flat monthly rent-the-site retainer.

Lead pattern to sell against: a new aerobic spray system owner needing a yearly maintenance
contract (wants the agreement emailed), a home buyer needing a service-contract quote after
inspection, a Canyon Lake homeowner asking for a maintenance-agreement price plus the call-out fee
outside the quarterly check, one sewage backup into a house. Recurring maintenance contracts
dominate, the most sellable lead type in the portfolio.

Notes on the research: our own site (Hill Country Aerobic Septic) shows up in every search for this
footprint and is not a candidate. Page-1 checks came from a search API, not a live Google SERP, so
"not on page 1" is approximate until Ara checks on his phone. Comal County hosts no maintenance-
provider roster (it defers to TCEQ); Guadalupe County does, and that roster is what confirmed the
MP numbers below.

## Licensing (verify by hand before offering anything)

- TCEQ licenses the person, not the company: OSSF Maintenance Provider (MP), Installer I/II (OS), Site Evaluator, Maintenance Technician (MT). Portal: [TCEQ licensing search](https://www2.tceq.texas.gov/lic_dpa/index.cfm). County roster: the [group search](https://www2.tceq.texas.gov/lic_dpa/index.cfm?fuseaction=licall.searchgp) with Program = OSSF, Type and Level = OSSF Maintenance Provider, County = Comal. By name: the [individual search](https://www2.tceq.texas.gov/lic_dpa/index.cfm?fuseaction=licall.searchindiv). The form is not fetchable by URL; run it by hand.
- Comal County hosts no MP roster. Its [OSSF Information page](https://www.comalcounty.gov/276/OSSF-Information) links to the TCEQ search and hosts only installer and site-evaluator lists. County engineer's office 830-221-1100.
- Guadalupe County (covers half of New Braunfels) publishes a roster with MP numbers, revised 6/25/2024: [Guadalupe MP list](https://www.guadalupetx.gov/upload/page/0267/docs/7-List%20of%20Maintenance%20Providers%206-25-24.pdf). Source for every MP number above.
- MP confirmed via that roster: Clear Choice (Trevino), Baker, Central Texas Septic & Aerobics, Centex Hydro-Flo, Wilton Krause, Countryside, Aerobic Services of South Texas, Block Creek, Lone Star, Advanced Aerobic Repair. Listed without a number: Gruene Aerobic, Luna. Not on any roster found: SOTX, Sargent, Andy's, Weidner. Check those four in the TCEQ individual search first.
